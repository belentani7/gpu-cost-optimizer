    METHOD & LIMITS

    A planning estimate, with its assumptions in view.

    Stage times scale from a fixed A100-class reference using image megapixels, scheduler steps, batch size, and published FP16 throughput figures. Estimated VRAM uses a simple resolution and batch heuristic. Neither formula models a specific checkpoint, runtime, attention backend, quantization, or provider setup.

    Cost is simulated seconds multiplied by a bundled hourly reference, divided by 3,600. The values in this release are historical 2024–2025 examples. They do not include storage, network, startup time, discounts, taxes, or provider billing granularity.

    Optimization and precision changes can affect compatibility and output quality. Validate recommendations with your own benchmark and current provider prices before using them in a production budget.
